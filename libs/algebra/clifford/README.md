# Clifford algebras and Clifford groups

`Field[K, field].On[V, space].Of[form]` constructs Clifford algebras from bilinear forms via \(q(v)=\operatorname{form}(v,v)\).
Quotient the noncommutative free associative coefficient-ring algebra by generator linearity and \(\iota(v)^2=q(v)1\).

| Module / declaration | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `Of.Carrier` / `Of.algebra` | Quotient carrier and associative-algebra structure |
| `Of.iota` / `Of.injection` | Canonical vector inclusion and linearity |
| `Of.cliffordRelation` | \(\iota(v)^2=q(v)1\) |
| `Of.Universal` | Unique homomorphisms from central coefficient maps and linear generator images |
| `Of.Relations` | Polarization anticommutation relations, triple products of generators |
| `Of.Grading` | Involutive grading automorphism negating generators, action on units, twisted adjoint |
| `Of.commutatorLieAlgebra` | Commutator Lie algebra |
| `Of.Pin` | Pin/Spin, complex extensions, group structures |
| `Of.Pin.Grading` / `Of.Pin.Reflection` | Involution preserves Pin and fixes Spin; twisted-adjoint reflection formulas for positive/negative unit vectors |
| `Circle` | Group \(U(1)\) of complex numbers satisfying \(z\overline z=1\) |
| `ComplexExtension.By` | \((G\times U(1))/\langle(-1,-1)\rangle\) using a central order-\(2\) element |
| `Field.Block` | Clifford block operators over coefficient algebras, linearity and square formulas |
| `Euclidean[n]` / `Euclidean.Successor` | Standard-inner-product Clifford algebra acting on two copies of the preceding algebra |

`Of.injection` names the canonical map as a linear map.
For standard inner products, `EuclideanFaithfulness.faithful n` gives injectivity of scalars and generators.
In dimension zero use the universal map to reals; inductively recover coefficients and leading/remaining vector coordinates by acting on constant block vectors.
Universality follows from quotient construction.
`Of.Induction` gives structural induction on scalars, generators, and algebra operations; `Of.HomExt` gives homomorphism uniqueness from agreement on generators.
`Euclidean.Successor.NormalForm` proves existence/uniqueness of \(j(a)+j(b)e_0\); `Spanning.recover` reconstructs it.
`EuclideanCoordinates.coordinates` recursively constructs real coefficient coordinates from normal forms; `injective` proves injectivity.
`NormalForm.Grading` and `NormalForm.Reversion` give two-component grading-automorphism and antiautomorphism formulas.
`EuclideanCenter.law` proves the twisted centralizer of generating vectors consists only of scalars.
`Of.Reversion` fixes scalars/vectors and reverses product order.
`Euclidean.PinNorm.norm` proves reversion equals inverse on Pin; `scalarSign` restricts scalar elements to \(\pm1\).
`Relations.anticommutator` derives the following without extra characteristic assumptions:
\[\iota(u)\iota(v)+\iota(v)\iota(u)=\bigl(\operatorname{form}(u,v)+\operatorname{form}(v,u)\bigr)1\]
`Grading.map` fixes coefficients and sends generators to \(-\iota(v)\); `involutive` proves its square is identity.
`Grading.action g x` is \(\alpha(g)xg^{-1}\); group multiplication agrees with action composition.

Pin is generated by units corresponding to vectors with \(q(v)=1\) or \(q(v)=-1\), together with scalar \(-1\).
`Euclidean.PinWords` proves unit-vector word representations of Pin elements.
`PinWords.Parity.fixedIff` identifies the even part with the fixed part of the grading automorphism.
Spin is generated by even products of unit vectors and \(-1\).
Explicit scalar signs ensure the convention includes \(\{\pm1\}\) even in dimension zero.
`Pin.inclusion` is the inclusion from Spin to Pin.
`PinC` and `SpinC` quotient products by diagonal signs; products/inverses are proved representative-independent.
`ComplexExtension.By.Lift` gives universality for maps out of this quotient.
`ComplexRepresentation` faithfully represents complex extensions of groups embedded in Pin by two Clifford components.
`Algebra` gives polynomial products/inverses; `Phase` recovers squared phase components from the two components.

`Euclidean[n]` in `geometry/lie_groups` supplies the standard inner product.
This specialization uses \(v^2=\|v\|^2\), so `Pin` is \(\mathrm{Pin}^{+}(n)\).
Negative forms give the \(v^2=-\|v\|^2\) convention.

`group` constructed here is a group structure.
This package does not construct finite-dimensional atlases, covering maps to orthogonal groups, or Lie group structures.

[Build and check](../../USAGE.md) from the repository root:

```sh
cargo run --manifest-path ref-type/Cargo.toml -p cli -- libs/algebra/clifford --module clifford
```

Standard inner products use \(\iota(v)^2=\lVert v\rVert^2\).
`PinAtlas.Dimension[n]`, `SpinGroup[n]`, `PinC[n]`, `SpinC[n]` in `geometry/lie_groups` construct the actual smooth manifolds and Lie group structures.
