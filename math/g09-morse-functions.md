# G09 Morse functions and noncritical intervals

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `morse_functions`.
- Prove existence of Morse functions with distinct critical values on compact manifolds and relative versions with prescribed boundary collars.
- Construct gradient flows and sublevel diffeomorphisms on noncritical intervals, including solution-existence time bounds.
- Prove finiteness of critical points, perturbations separating critical values, and boundary flow conditions needed for handle decomposition.
