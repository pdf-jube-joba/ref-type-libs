# T04 Pontryagin–Thom isomorphism

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `pontryagin_thom`.
- Construct smooth finite Grassmannians and universal bundles from graph coordinates; compare existing topological presentations.
- Smooth finite-stage Thom maps near zero sections and make them transverse; construct stable normal bundles on preimages.
- Construct cobordisms from homotopies by relative transversality; prove inverse-choice independence.
- Prove both composites with collapse are identities; expose graded ring isomorphisms with MO, MSO, and the sphere spectrum.
