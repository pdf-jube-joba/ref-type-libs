# K12 Comparing the Chern character and Thom classes

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `k_cohomology_comparison`.
- Descend Chern character to K-groups; construct natural multiplicative maps for relative and compactly supported groups.
- Prove that the Chern character of a complex bundle E's K-theoretic Thom class equals its ordinary Thom class times \(\operatorname{Td}(E)^{-1}\).
- Prove rational Chern-character isomorphisms for finite CW pairs and Riemann–Roch comparison formulas for embedding pushforwards.
