# L04 Symmetric polynomials and characteristic series

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: [`characteristic_series`](../libs/algebra/characteristic_series/README.md) ([notes](package-notes/algebra/characteristic_series.md)).
- Construct rational symmetric polynomials, elementary symmetric functions, and Newton identities.
- Construct truncated formal-series sums, products, inverses, composition, and exponentials; prove truncation-order compatibility.
- Expose Chern-character, Todd, L, and A-hat coefficients and multiplicative sequences as finite-degree polynomials; compute through degrees four and eight.

## Dependencies

None.
