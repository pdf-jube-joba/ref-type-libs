## Construction

Entry: `compact_support_k.Support`.
Actually construct one-point compactifications of locally compact Hausdorff spaces and apply K09 integer groups.

`OnePoint.Open` requires open original-space preimages, with compact complements when infinity is included.
Compactness takes one infinity-covering open and finite subcovers of its compact complement.
Separate finite points by original Hausdorffness; finite/infinite points by compact neighborhoods.

`On.Symbols` bundles live on compactifications; triple isomorphisms initially live at infinity.
B09 extends isomorphisms nearby; neighborhood complements are compact supports.
`Supported.With.Outside` restricts isomorphisms outside arbitrary larger compact supports.
`Complexes.TwoTerm` stores triples, global extensions of both maps, and finite embeddings.
`Supported.Complex.With.differential` is a global differential; `inverseAtInfinity`, `forwardEquation` give its invertibility outside support.

`On.Open.At` domains are groups with original-open subspace topologies.
`Coordinates` identifies compactification-open presentations, composing extensions/collapse.
`RepresentedAt` is the presentation before coordinate changes.
External products likewise compare compactification-axis complements with ordinary \(X\times Y\) via `On.Product.Coordinates.homeomorphism`.

Supply projection presentations to `Disk`.
For general bundles, choose `r` with `relative_k_theory.Relative.BundleDisk.existence` and pass `r.presentation`.
`BundleDisk.With[r].OriginalUnit` gives original-unit-disk homeomorphisms and sphere conditions.

### General bundle triples on original spaces

`On.BundleModel.From` accepts arbitrary locally trivial complex bundles with the specified base topology.
`On.BundleTriples[F, G, firstModel, secondModel].Supported[K, compactK].With[alpha]` takes two original-space bundles and an isomorphism outside compact \(K\).
No individual compactification-extension data required.

`With.In[n]` selects open \(U\), compact \(L\), \(K\subset U\subset L\), restricts bundles, and forms relative triples on \((L,L\setminus U)\).
Pullback via `On.Localization.collapse` extends relative classes by zero to \(X^+\).
`With.Choices.equation` proves independence using neighborhood-open intersections and compact unions.
`relative_k_theory.Relative.Pair.BundleTriples` ensures independence of finite projections and negative auxiliary complements.

`With.value` is the independent compact-support class; `With.symbol` an existing small-symbol element.
`With.comparison` identifies individual classes; `finiteRepresentation` gives compactification-triple representatives.
`On.Symbols.Supported.With.Raw.equation` proves restriction/reconstruction of existing symbols recovers original classes.
`On.Symbols.RawRepresentatives.existence` proves every class has original-space representatives.
Compactification representative equality can be tested by `On.Symbols.EqualityCriterion`.

`On.BundleTriples.To.Supported.With.equation` allows bundle-isomorphic presentation changes commuting outside support unions.
`With.Enlarge.comparison` restricts outside enlarged supports; `equation`, `symbolEquation` agree with original classes/symbols.
`On.Pullback.BundleTriples.Supported.With.naturality` proves general triples commute with proper pullback.

### Original-space triple relation presentations

`On.Triples.Group` generators are supported original-space triples.
`Relation` includes global isomorphisms, compatible bundle isomorphisms, sums, composition, continuous outside-support isomorphism homotopies.
`Equivalent` is equivalence generated by these and abelian operations, without defining via K-class equality.
`Triples.Input` moves arbitrary locally trivial bundles into coordinate models, giving choice-independent group elements.
`InputSum`, `InputComposition`, `Input.Homotopy` preserve original-bundle geometric relations.
`DirectSumInput.With.Stabilization` stabilizes by adding globally isomorphic diagonal triples.

`Group.ToK` adds via compact-neighborhood restrictions/zero extensions.
`FromCompact` and `Group.FromSymbols` compare compactification representatives with these classes; `Group.FromK.LeftInverse` proves the inverse comparison.
Inverse comparisons add complements to negative local-model bundles, descending through quotient spaces using boundary frames.
`Triples.Normalize` compares descended pullbacks with originals by gluing local stable and outside-support isomorphisms.
`Group.Comparison` gives additive two-sided inverses; `Group.EqualityCriterion` equates K-equality with generated geometric equivalence.
`On.Pullback.Group` constructs proper pullback; `On.Pullback.BundleTriples.Supported.With.GroupNaturality.equation` agrees with actual bundle-triple pullback.

### General finite complexes

`On.FiniteComplexes.Complex` accepts arbitrary finite bundle/differential sequences.
Foundational `bundle_operations.Operations.Over.Models.Chains.Data` has no length bound; `fold` constructs parity sums and both differentials.
Bundles are locally trivial coordinate models; ranks may vary pointwise.
`Models.From.existence` globally identifies arbitrary locally trivial bundles with coordinate models.
Transporting total topology avoids any global triviality requirement.

`FiniteComplexes.Of.Supported` assumes exactness outside compact sets.
`In[w]` constructs continuous compact-boundary contractions, normalized parity isomorphisms, relative triples, zero-extended K-classes.
`In.Choices` proves contraction independence; `Choices` compact-neighborhood independence.
`Enlarge.With.equation` proves support-enlargement invariance.
`Global.equation` proves globally exact finite complexes have zero classes.

Supply actual parity-sum isomorphisms commuting with differentials to `FiniteComplexes.Sum.With`.
`Supported.equation` gives sum additivity; `Supported.Stabilization.equation` stabilization by globally exact complexes.
`FiniteComplexes.Homotopy.With.Supported.In.With` takes continuous boundary-differential families on common compact neighborhoods.
Families are exact at each time; endpoint differentials match both finite complexes' parity differentials.
Identify endpoint parity bundles by specified global isomorphisms.
`equation` constructs normalized endpoint homotopies from compact-cylinder contractions, proving equal K-classes.
`Homotopy.With.Constant.Supported.equation` uses differential-compatible global parity isomorphisms as constant families, proving coordinate invariance.

`Of.Supported.Representatives` connects original-space formal triple representatives of finite complexes to geometric equality criteria.
`existence` gives formal representatives; `criterion` equates generated representative equivalence with K-equality.
