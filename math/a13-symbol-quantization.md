# A13 Symbols and local quantization

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `pseudodifferential_symbols`.
- Define matrix-valued symbols \(S^m_{1,0}\) on Euclidean open sets and classical homogeneous expansions; prove derivative estimates.
- Construct oscillatory integrals as cutoff limits and properly supported quantization operators acting on test functions and distributions.
- Expose smoothing operators, principal symbols, agreement with differential operators, and realization of asymptotic series.

- [A06 Schwartz spaces and the Fourier transform](a06-schwartz-fourier.md)
- [A08 Distributions and weak derivatives](../../libs/analysis/distributions/README.md)
