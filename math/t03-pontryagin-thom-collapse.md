# T03 Pontryagin–Thom collapse maps

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `pontryagin_thom_collapse`.
- Construct sphere-to-universal-Thom maps from closed-manifold normal classifying maps and tubular neighborhoods.
- Prove embedding/tubular/classifying-map choice independence and homotopies induced by cobordisms.
- Expose sum/product-compatible homomorphisms for unoriented, oriented, and framed cases.
