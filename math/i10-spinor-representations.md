# I10 Complex spinor representations

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: [`spinor_representations`](../libs/algebra/spinor_representations/README.md).
- Construct creation/annihilation representations on complex exterior algebras from existing Clifford algebras.
- Prove parity gradings, Hermitian inner products, and Clifford-adjoint and square formulas.
- Compare Spin/Spin-c representations, graded tensor products for direct sums, and exterior-algebra presentations after choosing complex structures.
- Construct the standard \(U(n)\to\operatorname{Spin}^c(2n)\) homomorphism and determinant correspondence for transfer to almost-complex-manifold bundles.

## Dependencies

- [Hermitian matrices and continuous local frames](../libs/algebra/hermitian_frames/README.md)
