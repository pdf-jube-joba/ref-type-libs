# C05 Projective bundle theorem and splitting principle

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `projective_bundle_cohomology`.
- Construct real/complex projective and flag bundles; prove that powers of canonical-line-bundle classes form cohomology bases.
- Prove injectivity of flag-bundle pullback and splitting into line bundles; expose naturality and iterated-pullback compatibility.
- Construct finite CW types of projectivizations and flag bundles; supply bases and relations to existing singular theory.
