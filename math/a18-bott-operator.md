# A18 Analytic normalization of the Bott operator

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `bott_operator`.
- Analyze the Euclidean harmonic oscillator using Gaussian and Hermite functions; prove completeness and closed-operator domain results.
- Construct the graded Bott operator on exterior algebras; compute its kernel, cokernel, and index 1.
- Expose the product formula for direct sums of dimensions and local operator expressions for subsequent Thom-map comparisons.

- [A07 Plancherel and Fourier multipliers](../libs/analysis/fourier_l2/README.md)
- [A11 Fredholm operators and the index](a11-fredholm-operators.md)
