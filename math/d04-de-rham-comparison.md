# D04 De Rham comparison theorem

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `de_rham_comparison`.
- Compare compact smooth manifolds and their bundle total spaces, including boundaries and compact supports.
- Construct smooth/continuous singular-chain comparisons and the local Poincaré lemma.
- Prove integration induces a natural isomorphism of real de Rham and singular cohomology.
- Prove product, relative, and compact-support comparisons and identify fundamental-class evaluation with integration of top forms.
