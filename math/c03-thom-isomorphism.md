# C03 Thom classes and the Thom isomorphism

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: [`cohomology_thom`](../libs/topology/cohomology_thom/README.md) ([notes](package-notes/topology/cohomology_thom.md)).
- Construct mod 2 and oriented integral Thom classes for real bundles over finite CW bases from fiber generators.
- Prove Thom isomorphisms by local trivializations and relative Mayer–Vietoris; handle arbitrary coefficient-ring changes.
- Prove pullback, direct-sum, orientation-change, and zero-section comparisons; expose Euler classes and sphere-bundle Gysin sequences.

- [C02 Disk bundles, sphere bundles, and Thom spaces](../libs/topology/thom_spaces/README.md)
- [C01 Cap products and relative products](../libs/topology/singular/cap/README.md)
