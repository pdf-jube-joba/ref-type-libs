# K08 Bott's basic product theorem

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: [`bott_product`](../libs/topology/bott_product/README.md) ([notes](package-notes/topology/bott_product.md)).
- Identify the complex projective line with the 2-sphere; construct the Hopf line H and \(u=[H]-1\).
- For compact Hausdorff X, prove a natural ring isomorphism \(K^0(X)[u]/(u^2)\cong K^0(X\times S^2)\) using standard external products and explicit inverses.
- Compare basepoint restrictions, relative groups, and signs from choices of Hopf line bundle.
