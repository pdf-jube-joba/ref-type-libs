# T05 Cobordism invariance of characteristic numbers

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `bordism_invariants`.
- Prove boundary Stiefel–Whitney/Pontryagin numbers vanish using boundary fundamental classes and stable tangent restrictions.
- Expose rational characteristic numbers/multiplicative genera on oriented cobordism and mod 2 numbers on unoriented cobordism.
- Prove signature cobordism invariance from metabolic boundary intersection forms.
