# A16 Elliptic parametrices and regularity

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `elliptic_parametrix`.
- Asymptotically construct left and right parametrices from principal symbols invertible off the cotangent zero section.
- Glue local constructions on closed compact manifolds; prove that both errors are smoothing operators.
- Prove elliptic estimates and regularity of distributional solutions; expose smoothness of solutions with smooth right-hand sides.
