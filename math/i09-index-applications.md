# I09 Gauss–Bonnet, signature, and Dirac index formulas

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `index_applications`.
- Compute Chern characters of de Rham, signature, and Spin-c Dirac symbols; connect to the general index formula.
- Prove Gauss–Bonnet via Euler classes, Hirzebruch signature via L classes, and Spin-c Dirac index via A-hat, determinant lines, and auxiliary bundles.
- Compute applicable formulas for spheres, tori, and the complex projective plane; expose agreement with characteristic numbers, cobordism invariants, and curvature integrals.
