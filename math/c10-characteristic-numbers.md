# C10 Characteristic numbers and concrete manifolds

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `characteristic_numbers`.
- Construct Stiefel–Whitney, Pontryagin, and stably complex Chern numbers by evaluation on closed-manifold fundamental classes.
- Compare smooth/topological real and complex projective-space models; compute characteristic numbers from stable tangent presentations.
- Expose compatibility with products, orientation reversal, and stable-normal computations, plus explicit low-dimensional nonzero values.
