# D05 Agreement of topological characteristic classes and characteristic forms

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `characteristic_form_comparison`.
- For smooth complex bundles, compare Chern–Weil classes with real images of integral Chern classes via the de Rham isomorphism.
- Extend to Pontryagin and Euler classes; check canonical-line-bundle normalization and orientation signs.
- Expose Todd, L, and A-hat characteristic numbers as integrals of curvature forms.
