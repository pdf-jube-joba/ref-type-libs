# B05 Finite Grassmannians and universal bundles

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `grassmannians`.
- Construct real and complex Grassmannians from orthogonal projections; expose graph coordinates, compact Hausdorffness, and tautological bundles.
- Prove dimension/ambient-dimension stabilization, direct-sum and orthogonal-complement maps, and pullback isomorphisms of universal bundles.
- Expose rank-one cases as real and complex projective spaces; construct canonical line bundles and their orthogonal complements.

## Dependencies

- [Bundle metrics, complements, and isomorphism classes](../../libs/geometry/bundle_complements/README.md)
