# I03 Analytic index and Thom maps

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `analytic_index_thom`.
- Prove operator realizations and index product formulas for external products of symbols and Bott operators.
- Prove complex-normal-bundle Thom maps commute with the analytic index using local trivializations and excision.
- Apply to complex structures on normal bundles induced on cotangent bundles by real-manifold embeddings; expose index preservation under embeddings.
