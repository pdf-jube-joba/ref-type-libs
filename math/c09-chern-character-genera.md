# C09 Chern character and multiplicative characteristic classes

## Requirements

- Primary implementation: `characteristic_genera`.
- Construct Chern character, Todd, L, and A-hat classes as rational singular-cohomology elements on finite CW spaces.
- Prove Chern-character additivity for sums and multiplicativity for tensors, and direct-sum/virtual-difference formulas for multiplicative classes.
- Handle finite-dimensional series truncation and rank-zero terms; compute on canonical and tangent bundles of projective spaces.
